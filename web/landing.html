INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK

Use @finfolk.com.au.

Connect Instagram, check in by message and share posts from Instagram or TikTok.

04 / INSTAGRAM COMMANDS

Connect. Check in and out.

Follow @finfolk.com.au and send /connect on Instagram. Enter the code in FinFolk, then message /in Brighton Beach, VIC when you arrive and /out when you leave.

Commands are available when Instagram messaging is enabled. Arrival and departure record your visit. Dive logs are managed separately.

05 / INSTAGRAM POSTS & REELS

Tag your session.

Mention @finfolk.com.au in your public post or Reel. Copy its link, choose the location in FinFolk and submit your post for review.

You confirm that the post is yours and consent to public sharing. It appears after review.

06 / TIKTOK VIDEOS

Bring your video.

Mention @finfolk.com.au in your public TikTok video. Copy the full video link and submit it with its location through Your posts in FinFolk.

TikTok posts use link submission. You confirm ownership and public sharing before review.

07 / REGULARS

Become a Regular.

Check in on 10 separate days within the last 30 days to become a Regular at a location. Find other freedivers and view their shared Instagram and TikTok profiles.

  1. 01Check in
  2. 02Keep coming back
  3. 0310th day: become a Regular

Visits are self-reported. You choose your profile and what you share with other divers.
